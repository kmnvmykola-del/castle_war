## Game name

Frontier Kingdoms

## Game description

Command your army and conquer the frontier!

Capture villages, train troops, upgrade your strongholds and fight for control of the roads in a medieval strategy game. Keep your army supplied, defend your kingdom and choose the right moment to attack.

⚔️ Battle AI on three difficulty levels or challenge players in casual and ranked 1v1 matches.
🏰 Command four unit types and develop seven building types through three upgrade levels.
🎨 Collect 25 complete cosmetic sets for your army, buildings and roads. Cosmetics do not change combat stats.
🗺️ Create your own maps and test them against AI in the built-in Map Editor.

Desktop: left-click to select a building, right-click to send troops. Hold Shift to select multiple friendly buildings. Use WASD to move the camera and the mouse wheel to zoom.
Mobile: tap to select a building, then use Order to send troops. Drag to move the camera and pinch to zoom.

Early access. Feedback is welcome!
